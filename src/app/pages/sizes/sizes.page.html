How to measure
  • Use a soft tape and measure over light clothing; keep the tape snug, not tight.
  • Bust, waist, hip: around the fullest or narrowest part, tape parallel to the floor.
  • Front length and trouser length: from the shoulder or waist down to where you want the hem.
  • Not sure? Leave it blank. Our master tailor will confirm before cutting.
